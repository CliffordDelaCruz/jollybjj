Welcome to JollyBJJ!

Welcome to JollyBJJ — the chillest Jiujitsu crew inspired by good food, good rolls, and good friends. If you love Jollibee, Jiujitsu, or just meeting awesome people, you’re already one of us!

Hi! My name is Clifford, the founder of JollyBJJ. I am a Jiujitsu black belt under Robyn Goudy of Matlife and Zorobabel Moreira and Thiago Gaspary of FaMA. Before moving to New Zealand, I also trained at Singapore Submission Grappling under the guidance of Chance Wanlass and David Ooi.

Since its founding in 2024, my goal has been to:

Train and share ideas that will cultivate growth to all members.

Share the passion for anything food related, especially the Philippines' favorite fried chicken!

Make friends from all parts of the world! We set aside political and cultural differences.
